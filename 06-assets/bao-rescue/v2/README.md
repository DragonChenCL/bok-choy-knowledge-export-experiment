# Bao Rescue v2 - Visual Diagnostic Prototype

Date: 2026-09-07
Status: REVIEW PROTOTYPE

## What changed from v1

v1 was rejected because it behaved like a text-heavy article placed inside a PDF.

v2 is rebuilt around visual diagnosis:

1. Cover contains a symptom mosaic rather than a text-first introduction.
2. Page 2 is a visual symptom atlas.
3. Each major failure pattern gets its own image-first page.
4. Every diagnostic page is reduced to:
   - what it looks like;
   - what to check first;
   - likely cause families;
   - one controlled next-batch test;
   - what NOT to change yet.
5. Page 9 is a 30-second decision tree.
6. Page 10 is a next-batch worksheet + evidence note.

## Current visual set

The prototype contains custom diagnostic illustrations for:

- wrinkled / elephant-skin surface;
- collapsed after steaming;
- dense / tight crumb;
- wet / pitted condensation damage;
- barely risen / under-proofed state;
- spread / fragile / over-proofed state.

The images are intentionally labeled as **diagnostic illustrations**. They are not presented as photographs of real customer batches.

## Commercial-image rule

Do not copy community / Reddit / recipe-site photos into the paid product without permission.

Commercial v2 can use:

1. original project photography;
2. custom diagnostic illustrations;
3. AI-generated standardized diagnostic visuals, clearly represented as illustrations where appropriate;
4. licensed or permission-cleared photography.

## Quality check performed

The 10-page PDF was rendered to page images and visually inspected after generation.

Checks:

- no clipping or obvious overlap;
- images occupy the main visual area on diagnostic pages;
- symptom pages do not revert to article-length prose;
- the first two pages support fast pattern matching;
- the one-variable troubleshooting rule is repeated at the action point, not buried in notes.

## Remaining review question

This is a **v2 prototype for product-direction review**, not yet automatically approved for sale.

The next decision should be based on whether a real buyer can look at the first two pages and say: "that is exactly what my bun looks like" within roughly 20-30 seconds.
