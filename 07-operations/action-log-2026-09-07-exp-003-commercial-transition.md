# Action Log — EXP-003 Transition to Minimum Commercial Validation

Date: 2026-09-07

## Action 1 — Re-check whether desk research supports a commercial test

### Why now
EXP-003 had already passed the internal research threshold for repeated pain, parent-market activity, current user questions and apparently fragmented troubleshooting supply. Before building anything, the commercial test needed a documented evidence gate.

### External evidence reviewed
- current Etsy Bao digital recipe listing around $4.53;
- current Etsy troubleshooting products in adjacent baking categories commonly in roughly the $5–$9 range, with stronger systems priced higher;
- current Bao troubleshooting pages from King Arthur, Omnivore's Cookbook, Red House Spice, Dished by Kate, What To Cook Today and others;
- repeated recent community failures around collapse, wrinkling, density and proofing.

### Observation
The evidence supports testing a compact paid troubleshooting format, but does not yet prove willingness to pay for Bao troubleshooting specifically.

### Decision
Move from pure desk research to **minimum commercial validation preparation**.

### Classification
- move to test: DECISION
- exact willingness to pay: UNKNOWN / HYPOTHESIS

---

## Action 2 — Set MVP scope

### Evidence
The research sample repeatedly clusters around:
1. wrinkled / collapsed buns;
2. dense / not fluffy buns;
3. proofing uncertainty.

### Decision
Version 1 will focus on these three families plus only a small number of supporting checks (steamer / condensation / flour / yeast / hydration).

### Why not build a full cookbook
Search results already contain abundant recipes. The observed opportunity is diagnostic compression, not recipe scarcity.

---

## Action 3 — Set initial format hypothesis

### Evidence
- adjacent digital troubleshooting products exist as compact PDFs / printables;
- problem structure is visual and decision-based;
- building a small product reduces sunk cost before commercial proof.

### Decision
Test an **8–12 page visual PDF + 1-page printable diagnostic**.

### Classification
FORMAT = HYPOTHESIS supported by adjacent-product evidence.

---

## Action 4 — Set initial price hypothesis

### Evidence
Observed price anchors:
- generic Bao recipe PDF: about $4.53;
- sourdough troubleshooting products: commonly around $4.99–$8.99 in current Etsy results;
- stronger troubleshooting systems / specialty diagnostic guides can exceed $10.

### Decision
Initial launch-test price: **$6.90 USD**.

### Important
This is not claimed as the market-clearing price. It is an experiment price selected inside an observed adjacent range and slightly above a basic recipe PDF because the offer is diagnostic rather than just a recipe.

---

## Action 5 — Connect product to acquisition before building

### Evidence
Manual SERP and user-question research repeatedly shows exact failure intents around:
- wrinkled steamed buns;
- Bao collapse after steaming;
- Bao not fluffy / dense.

### Decision
The first three commercial SEO entry pages will target these three failure families instead of the broad `bao buns` term.

### Reason
These users are already problem-aware and therefore closer to the Bao Rescue offer.

---

## Action 6 — Create a solution evidence quality gate

### Why
Search results contain conflicting advice because different steamed-bun formulas behave differently.

### Example
A current no-yeast steamed-bun troubleshooting page explicitly uses different heat logic from regular yeasted buns.

### Decision
Technical product claims must be separated by method family and evidence strength. Community discussion can define symptom language but cannot be treated as solution authority.
