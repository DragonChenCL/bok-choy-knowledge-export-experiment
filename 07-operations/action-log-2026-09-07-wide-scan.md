## Action 4 — Check cross-market knowledge advantage

### Why
The user identified a strategy of using mature Chinese traditional knowledge as a source advantage, provided the target-market context is revalidated.

### Observation
Bao / mantou production maps directly to mature Chinese practices around 发面 / 醒面, kneading/degassing, flour choice, proof-state recognition, steaming, condensation and post-steam cooling.

### Boundary
Chinese advice cannot be translated literally because:
- flour protein conventions differ;
- yeast products differ;
- home steamer hardware differs;
- climate differs;
- recipe goals differ between mantou, baozi and Western-style folded bao buns.

### Decision
Cross-market knowledge transfer is a **research advantage**, not proof of target-market demand.
