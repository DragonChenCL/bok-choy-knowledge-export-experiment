- no single specialist troubleshooting brand visibly dominates this result set.

## Observation

## Interpretation
For a low-cost experiment, exact Volume/KD/CPC are useful but not required to decide whether to run a minimal commercial test.

## Confidence
High for the methodological decision; low/unknown for exact search-size quantification.

## Decision
Do not pay for keyword API units solely to continue EXP-003. Continue with free/public evidence and retain exact keyword metrics as UNKNOWN.
