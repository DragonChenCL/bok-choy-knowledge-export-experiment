# Action Log — 2026-09-07 — EXP-003 Batch 01

## Action 1 — Validate parent-market size before deep troubleshooting research

### Why this action now
EXP-001 demonstrated that a real pain point can still be commercially too small. We therefore check the parent market before investing in deeper pain-point extraction.

### Action
Searched current public keyword / market / video evidence around `bao buns` and `steamed buns`.

### Result
Parent demand is materially larger than Bok-Choy-only. Public Semrush data shows `bao buns` ~60,500 US monthly searches (Jun 2026). Multiple bao / steamed-bun videos have 1M+ views.

### Decision
Continue.

---

## Action 2 — Manually inspect failure-query SERPs

### Why this action now
Parent demand alone does not prove the troubleshooting opportunity.

### Action
Reviewed representative failure searches for not-fluffy, collapse, wrinkle, dense, proofing, rising, flat, yellow spots.

### Result
Search supply is fragmented across generic recipe FAQs, small sites, YouTube and communities. Dedicated troubleshooting brands are not obviously dominant.

### Limitation
No reliable exact monthly volumes were found for the failure subqueries in this batch.

### Decision
Mark cluster demand `UNKNOWN`; do not substitute the 60,500 parent volume.

---

## Action 3 — Check recent real-user failures

### Why this action now
Need to verify that failure modes still occur in 2026, not only in old evergreen recipe content.

### Action
Reviewed recent Reddit and forum examples.

### Result
Current examples include wrinkling, dry dough / poor fluffiness, over-proofing from generic advice, dense/lumpy dough, and uncertainty about flour/kneading.

### Decision
Pain existence confirmed at anecdotal level; continue toward 30–50 sample.

---

## Action 4 — Scan paid-product competition

### Why this action now
A low-competition SEO gap is not automatically a paid-product opportunity.

### Action
Searched Etsy / Gumroad / Amazon-oriented web results for bao recipe PDFs and troubleshooting products.

### Result
Generic bao recipe PDFs are clearly offered for sale, often ~$2–5. No obvious mature troubleshooting-product category surfaced during this scan.

### Decision
Direct payment evidence for `failure diagnosis` remains weak. Keep as gap, not as conclusion.

---

## Action 5 — Check cross-market knowledge-source advantage

### Why this action now
The project hypothesis includes possible knowledge transfer from mature Chinese steamed-bread practice/research into an English diagnostic format.

### Action
Reviewed food-science sources on Chinese steamed bread production variables.

### Result
Technical literature explicitly controls proof temperature, humidity, flour properties, sheeting, proof endpoint and steaming. This offers more structured process knowledge than typical generic English FAQs.

### Decision
Create localization source map. Do not directly copy lab temperatures/times into consumer advice.
