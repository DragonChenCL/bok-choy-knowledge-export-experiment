# Action Log — EXP-003 Batch 03

Date: 2026-09-07

## Action 1 — Reduce Reddit bias and cross the minimum pain-sample gate

### Hypothesis / question
Are the same Bao failures visible in high-intent recipe-site readers and other cooking communities, not only Reddit?

### Why this action now
Batch 02 had ~19 unique cases and was still Reddit-heavy. The reusable SOP calls for 30–50 qualified problems where feasible and prefers multiple source types.

### Action
Reviewed public recipe comments and cooking-community pages, including Red House Spice and What To Cook Today, and added recent Reddit items only where they were distinct and useful.

### Raw evidence
Added 16 distinct records in Batch 03, bringing the combined working sample to approximately **35 unique qualified problems**.

Non-Reddit examples include:
- small-steamer batch timing / over-proofing;
- recipe scaling creating too-dry dough;
- local flour substitution (00 flour);
- Instant Pot timing creating brown/burnt buns;
- buns not soft enough;
- overnight dough-storage uncertainty;
- damp/translucent blotches;
- repeated wrinkling/collapse after many failed attempts;
- previous tough/chewy dough;
- sealing/pleating failures.

### Observation
The same failure categories recur outside Reddit.

### Interpretation
Pain clustering is likely real rather than platform-specific.

### Confidence
High for existence of repeated user problems; still not a measure of total market size.

### Decision
Mark the SOP `30+ user problem` gate as passed.

### Next evidence needed
Quantitative failure-query search demand and stronger commercial evidence.

## Action 3 — Payment / competitor map

### Hypothesis / question
Do people pay for Bao knowledge, and is failure diagnosis already a mature paid category?

### Action

### Raw evidence
Paid Bao/mantou recipe PDFs surfaced in low single-digit price ranges, including fresh 2026 listings. Broad cookbooks also exist. No obvious mature category of dedicated Bao troubleshooting flowcharts/rescue guides surfaced in this batch.

### Observation

### Decision
