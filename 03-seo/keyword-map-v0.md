# Keyword / Search-Intent Map v0

Status: **Exploratory**. This map records observed phrasing and SERP themes. It does not claim keyword search volume because no validated volume source has been collected yet.

## Principle
Prioritize symptom-first and decision-first queries because community evidence shows beginners usually describe what they see rather than naming the pest correctly.

## Cluster A — broad problem / hub intent

Candidate queries:
- bok choy pests
- bok choy pest problems
- common bok choy pests
- what is eating my bok choy

Current SERP observation:
- Results include university/agricultural sources, specialist gardening sites, and videos.
- The broad topic is clearly supplied with free content, so a paid product cannot win by simply repeating a pest list.

Proposed role:
- Hub / orientation page, not the primary paid-value proposition.

## Cluster B — holes / chewing damage

Candidate queries:
- bok choy leaves holes
- holes in bok choy leaves
- tiny holes in bok choy leaves
- large holes in bok choy leaves
- bok choy leaves eaten overnight
- what is eating my bok choy leaves

Observed evidence:
- Public search results include a dedicated "Holes in Bok Choy Leaves" article.
- Reddit examples show users frequently see damage without seeing the culprit.
- Specialist content maps large ragged holes to chewing pests and night activity can matter for slugs/snails.

Proposed role:
- High-priority symptom cluster.
- Potential SEO landing page + major branch in the paid decision guide.

## Cluster C — flea beetles / shot holes

Candidate queries:
- bok choy flea beetles
- flea beetle damage bok choy
- tiny round holes bok choy
- small black jumping bugs bok choy

Observed evidence:
- University of Kentucky explicitly includes bok choy among brassica crops affected by flea beetles.
- Kentucky crop handbook calls flea beetles a major bok choy pest.
- Ontario specialty-crop guidance also identifies flea beetles as significant.

Proposed role:
- Priority pest page.
- Strong candidate for a visual "tiny shot holes" diagnostic card.

## Cluster D — aphids / sticky-curled growth

Candidate queries:
- aphids on bok choy
- bok choy aphids
- bugs under bok choy leaves
- bok choy leaves curling aphids
- sticky bok choy leaves aphids

Observed evidence:
- NCSU, UF/IFAS and other agricultural sources list aphids among bok choy pests.
- Reddit discussions show beginners asking how to identify/remove aphids on bok choy and leafy greens.

Proposed role:
- Priority pest page and symptom branch.

## Cluster E — caterpillars / cabbage worms

Candidate queries:
- cabbage worms on bok choy
- caterpillars eating bok choy
- bok choy cabbage looper
- bok choy leaves skeletonized
- green worms on bok choy

Observed evidence:
- NCSU lists caterpillar pests including loopers/cutworms/armyworms.
- UF/IFAS lists cabbage caterpillars.
- Reddit users repeatedly identify chewing damage and ask whether plants can recover.

Proposed role:
- Priority pest page and "severe chewing / skeletonized leaves" branch.

## Cluster F — slugs / night damage

Candidate queries:
- slugs eating bok choy
- snails eating bok choy
- bok choy eaten overnight
- irregular holes bok choy leaves

Observed evidence:
- NCSU identifies slugs/snails as potential problems.
- Specialist bok choy pest guidance describes irregular holes, slime trails and night activity.

Proposed role:
- Priority diagnostic branch because "check at night" is an actionable confirmation step.

## Cluster G — rescue decision

Candidate queries:
- can bok choy recover from pest damage
- should I pull damaged bok choy
- will bok choy grow back after bugs eat it
- bok choy plant eaten can it recover

Observed evidence:
- Reddit user explicitly asks whether severely damaged baby bok choy can regrow or should be pulled.

Proposed role:
- Important **decision-intent** content.
- Potential paid-value differentiator: SAVE / WATCH / PULL framework.

## Cluster H — prevention / protection

Candidate queries:
- how to protect bok choy from pests
- how to keep bugs off bok choy
- bok choy row cover pests
- prevent flea beetles bok choy
- protect bok choy seedlings

Observed evidence:
- Extension sources discuss monitoring, sanitation, cultural practices, netting/row covers and other prevention approaches.
- Beginner Reddit discussion often shifts from identification to "how do I stop this next time?"

Proposed role:
- Free SEO page + prevention checklist / bonus asset.

## Cluster I — yellowing / non-pest troubleshooting

Candidate queries:
- bok choy leaves turning yellow
- why is my bok choy yellow
- bok choy leaf spots
- bok choy white spots

Observed evidence:
- SERPs include troubleshooting content and authoritative sources list disease issues alongside pests.

Proposed role:
- Defer from MVP unless question clustering shows meaningful demand.
- Important to avoid overdiagnosing every visual issue as insects.

## Current priority order for further validation

3. Caterpillars / large or skeletonized chewing
4. Aphids / leaf undersides / sticky-curled growth
5. Slugs/snails / overnight damage

7. Prevention / protection

- classify them into these clusters;
- quantify cluster frequency in our sample;
