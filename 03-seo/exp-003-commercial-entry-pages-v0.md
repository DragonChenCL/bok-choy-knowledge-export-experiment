# EXP-003 — First Commercial SEO Entry Pages

Date: 2026-09-07
Status: acquisition hypotheses for minimum commercial validation

## Principle
Do not begin with the broad `bao buns` term. Enter through exact failure intent where the user already has a problem and is looking for an explanation.

These pages were selected because the same problems recur across current SERPs, Reddit questions, recipe FAQs and recipe-comment histories.

---

## Page 1 — Why Do Steamed Buns Wrinkle?

### Primary intent
Diagnose wrinkled / elephant-skin steamed buns after steaming.

### Candidate queries
- why do steamed buns wrinkle
- steamed buns wrinkled after steaming
- why are my bao buns wrinkled
- bao elephant skin
- steamed bun rough surface

### Evidence supporting priority
- 2026 Reddit post specifically asking what causes steamed buns to wrinkle received strong engagement (~474 post upvotes in the observed snapshot).
- Multiple established recipe / troubleshooting pages explicitly discuss wrinkling / collapse.

### Page promise
Show the user how to distinguish:
- collapse immediately after lid opening;
- over-proofing;
- surface drying;
- aggressive steam;
- condensation-related surface damage.

### CTA
`Still not sure which failure path fits your batch? See the Bao Rescue decision guide.`

---

## Page 2 — Why Do Bao Buns Collapse After Steaming?

### Primary intent
User's buns looked inflated, then deflated / collapsed during or after steaming.

### Candidate queries
- bao buns collapse after steaming
- steamed buns collapse after steaming
- bao buns deflate
- why did my bao collapse
- steamed buns collapse when lid opens

### Evidence supporting priority
- King Arthur Baking warns rapid exposure to cooler air can contribute to collapse and recommends a covered post-steam rest for yeasted buns.
- Omnivore's Cookbook includes deflation as a recurring Bao troubleshooting problem and discusses over-proofing and pressure / temperature transitions.
- Recent English Bao guides continue to include collapse troubleshooting.

### Page promise
A short decision tree:
1. When did collapse happen?
2. What was the proof state before steaming?
3. How aggressive was steaming?
4. How was the lid opened / rested?
5. Was the surface wet from condensation?

### CTA
`Use the full Bao Rescue symptom tree to isolate the next variable to change.`

---

## Page 3 — Why Are My Bao Buns Not Fluffy?

### Primary intent
Dense, doughy, tough or heavy steamed buns.

### Candidate queries
- why are my bao buns not fluffy
- steamed buns not fluffy
- bao buns dense
- bao buns doughy
- steamed buns heavy
- bao buns tough

### Evidence supporting priority
- Wagamama and other current Bao pages explicitly answer variants of `why are my bao buns not fluffy?`.
- What To Cook Today maintains a dedicated troubleshooting section for steamed buns that are dense / not fluffy.
- Real-user cases repeatedly mention dense, pasta-like, lumpy or insufficiently fluffy results.

### Page promise
Help separate:
- yeast did not create enough gas;
- proofing did not progress enough;
- dough / flour structure problem;
- hydration / kneading issue;
- collapse after initially successful rise.

### CTA
`If your symptoms overlap, use the Bao Rescue dense/not-fluffy diagnostic.`

---

## Why only three pages first
This is an experiment, not a content farm.

The first three pages map directly to the three strongest recurring failure families found in current research:
1. wrinkle / collapse;
2. dense / not fluffy;
3. proofing uncertainty (embedded heavily in pages 1 and 2, with a dedicated page to be added only after traffic signals justify it).

Do not create 30 AI-generated articles before these pages show impressions / clicks / useful query data.

## Measurement after publication
For each page track:
- indexation;
- impressions;
- exact queries surfaced in Search Console;
- average position;
- clicks;
- CTA clicks to Bao Rescue;
- purchases / assisted purchases where measurable.

## Expansion rule
New pages should be created from:
- Search Console query evidence;
- repeated user questions not adequately served by an existing page;
- strong SERP gaps confirmed manually.

Not from AI keyword brainstorming alone.
