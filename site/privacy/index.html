Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bao Rescue handles information when you use bao.serunio.com. Bao Rescue is an online troubleshooting tool for people making steamed buns (bao) at home.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process the following information:

  • Diagnostic inputs: the symptoms, answers, and troubleshooting choices you provide while using Bao Rescue.
  • Optional contact or checkout information: such as an email address if you provide one during checkout or support.
  • Payment-related identifiers: order IDs, payment status, product purchased, purchase time, and similar transaction metadata received from our payment provider.
  • Technical and usage data: such as pages viewed, referral source, browser/device information, approximate location derived from network information, and interaction events used to understand product performance.

2. Payment information

Bao Rescue does not collect or store your full payment card number.

Payments are handled by Waffo Pancake or other payment providers we may use in the future. Payment providers may collect payment and identity information directly under their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Bao Rescue normally receives only the information needed to confirm a purchase and deliver the digital product.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide and improve the Bao Rescue diagnostic experience;
  • remember relevant troubleshooting state during a session;
  • confirm purchases and unlock paid digital content;
  • investigate failed payments, access problems, refunds, or abuse;
  • measure which pages and features are useful;
  • understand traffic sources and improve the website;
  • protect the service from fraud, misuse, and technical attacks.

4. Analytics and third-party services

Bao Rescue currently uses third-party services that may process technical and usage information on our behalf, including:

  • Google Analytics for website traffic and usage measurement;
  • Microsoft Clarity for understanding how visitors interact with pages and identifying usability problems;
  • Waffo Pancake for payment processing, checkout, transaction confirmation, and related merchant-of-record services;
  • hosting and infrastructure providers used to serve the website and payment-related backend services.

These providers may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, request logs, or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Bao Rescue and its analytics providers may use cookies, local storage, and session storage. These technologies may be used to maintain session state, remember where a diagnostic visit came from, measure page usage, and support analytics or payment flows.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain storage technologies may affect analytics or some product functionality.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Diagnostic session data may be temporary or stored in the browser. Payment and transaction records may be retained longer where needed for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, refunds, or legal obligations.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers when necessary to operate the website, process payments, analyze usage, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

8. International processing

Bao Rescue is an online service and some service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, providers are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also control analytics cookies and browser storage through your browser settings.

If you make a privacy request, we may need enough information to verify that the request relates to you.

10. Children

Bao Rescue is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are below the age at which they can lawfully consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to limit access to sensitive service credentials and payment-related systems. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through diagnostic fields.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Bao Rescue changes, for example when we add a new payment provider or analytics tool. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or other support needs, contact Bao Rescue at support@serunio.com. We will make a reasonable effort to respond to legitimate requests.
