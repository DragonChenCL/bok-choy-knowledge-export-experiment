# Decision Log

## 2026-09-07 — D001: Treat Bok Choy as an experiment, not a committed business

**Evidence:** recurring Reddit problem discussions + authoritative pest sources + existing paid pest-printable market.

**Decision:** proceed to structured validation.

**Why:** problem and format are plausible, but Bok-Choy-specific willingness-to-pay is not yet proven.

**Rejected alternative:** immediately write a full Asian Greens ebook.

---

## 2026-09-07 — D002: Organize around symptoms and decisions, not pest taxonomy

**Evidence:** Reddit users often describe holes/damage and explicitly say they cannot see or identify the pest. Etsy diagnostic-flowchart positioning also sells symptom-led decision compression.

**Decision:** product and SEO research will start from user-visible symptoms and decisions.

**Why:** this matches novice mental models better than alphabetical pest chapters.

**Rejected alternative:** conventional encyclopedia structure (Aphids → Flea Beetles → Slugs...).

---

## 2026-09-07 — D003: Do not produce the paid PDF yet

**Evidence gap:** no validated query volumes, no large enough user-question sample, no Bok-Choy-specific paid-demand proof.

**Decision:** next milestone is a 30–50+ question pain-point dataset plus representative SERP review.

**Why:** producing the PDF now would violate the evidence-first rule and risk optimizing a product before confirming the strongest problem cluster.

---

## 2026-09-07 — D004: Avoid universal pesticide instructions in MVP

**Evidence:** authoritative guidance varies by crop, region, registration and local recommendation. Extension sources encourage monitoring, cultural controls and correct identification.

**Decision:** initial content emphasizes diagnosis, inspection, cultural/physical/IPM-oriented steps, prevention, and escalation to current local Extension/product-label guidance.

**Why:** improves reliability and avoids unsafe overgeneralization.
