## Interpretation

By the reusable SOP, 13–15 means **promising, but gather missing evidence before build**.

The score must not be rounded up because the broad `bao buns` keyword is large. The mandatory missing variable is the actual demand for failure/troubleshooting intent.

## Current decision

# HOLD — evidence promising, but no paid-product build yet

### What would move this to PROCEED
At least one of the following should materially strengthen:

1. cluster-level search-demand evidence shows enough recurring failure-query traffic;
2. a small free diagnostic asset attracts meaningful clicks/downloads from relevant search/community distribution;
3. direct payment/preorder test shows willingness to pay for troubleshooting rather than recipes.

### What could trigger PIVOT
- failure-query demand proves too small: broaden from `Bao Failure Diagnosis` to `Chinese Steamed Bun / Mantou & Bao Troubleshooting`;
- users care much more about proofing than other failure types: narrow into a `Bao Proofing Decision Guide`;
- equipment-specific questions dominate: consider `Steamed Buns in Western Home Kitchens` or an equipment-localization angle.

## Important strategic finding

The strongest differentiated hypothesis is not “China has a better bao recipe.” It is:

> English home cooks face multiple interacting variables and fragmented advice; Chinese steamed-bread process knowledge may allow a more useful diagnostic framework after localization.

That hypothesis is now evidence-supported enough to keep researching, not enough to sell as fact.
