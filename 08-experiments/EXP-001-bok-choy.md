# EXP-001 — Bok Choy Pest Rescue

## Experiment question
Can a zero-audience operator create and sell a useful English digital knowledge product that helps beginner home gardeners diagnose and respond to bok choy pest damage?

## Current stage
Problem / market validation.

## Primary user hypothesis
Beginner or early-stage home gardeners growing bok choy who see visible damage but cannot confidently identify the cause or decide what to do next.

## Job to be done
> "Something is damaging my bok choy. Help me narrow the likely cause, confirm it, and decide what to do next without spending hours searching conflicting advice."

## Product hypothesis
A compact visual, symptom-first decision guide / printable that organizes reliable guidance as:

**damage pattern → likely causes → confirmation checks → immediate next steps → recovery / prevention decision**

Potential differentiator:
- symptom-first, not pest-name-first;
- SAVE / WATCH / PULL decision support;
- strong sourcing to Extension / authoritative guidance;
- intentionally avoids pretending to give universal pesticide instructions.

## Evidence supporting the experiment
1. Current Reddit discussions show repeated bok choy damage-identification questions, including cases where the user cannot see the pest.
2. Authoritative agricultural sources independently confirm multiple common bok choy pest categories.
3. Current Etsy listings demonstrate that pest-identification flowcharts and garden-pest printables are offered as paid digital products, including a symptom-led diagnostic product listed at $11.99.
4. Broad free content already exists, so the paid product must compress decision-making rather than merely aggregate pest facts.

## Evidence NOT yet established
- Bok-choy-specific paid demand.
- Search volume of the main symptom queries.
- Best acquisition channel for the first stranger purchase.
- Optimal product depth / page count / price.

## Falsification conditions
Do not continue merely because the guide is easy to produce.

Pause / change direction if:
- user-question collection shows little repeated bok choy-specific pain;
- SERPs fully satisfy symptom queries with highly usable free decision tools;
- marketplace research shows no meaningful differentiation opportunity;
- early free lead magnet attracts no relevant traffic after a reasonable distribution test;
- product page receives qualified traffic but no conversion after pricing / positioning iterations.

## Success ladder
1. 30–50 qualified real-user questions collected and clustered.
2. 3+ repeated high-intent problem clusters verified.
3. Free lead magnet created around the strongest cluster.
4. First qualified organic / community visitor.
5. First lead-magnet download from a stranger.
6. First product-page visit from a stranger.
7. **First payment from a stranger.**

Only after step 7 do we discuss portfolio-scale replication.
