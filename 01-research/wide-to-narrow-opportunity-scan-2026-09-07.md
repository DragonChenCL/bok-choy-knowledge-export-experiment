# Wide-to-Narrow Opportunity Scan — 2026-09-07

## Objective
Starting from broad, low-compliance knowledge categories, identify vertical problems that show **strong audience attention / repeated pain** but **weaker or less-obvious direct paid competition**.

This scan is exploratory. It is designed to create candidates for the full Topic Validation SOP, not to approve a product automatically.

---

## Scan rule
For each candidate, check at least three kinds of evidence:

1. **Attention / pain** — recent Reddit/forum questions, engagement, YouTube views, recurring failure language.
2. **Search demand proxy** — Google/Semrush/public keyword evidence where available.
3. **Paid competition** — Etsy / paid guides / calculators / courses.
4. **SERP structure** — whether exact problem queries are dominated by strong dedicated competitors or are still mainly answered inside generic articles/FAQ sections.
5. **Cross-market knowledge advantage** — whether mature knowledge from China/other markets might improve the solution after localization.

---

# Candidate set

## A. Sourdough troubleshooting

### Evidence
- Multiple 2026 Etsy troubleshooting PDFs already exist, including one-page and multi-page products priced around $1.99–$4.99+.
- Recent public community questions continue around starter failure, fermentation, temperature, etc.

### Observation
Demand and willingness-to-pay are well proven.

### Competition
**High / obvious.** Many direct troubleshooting PDFs already exist.

### Decision
Keep as benchmark market, but it is not our best example of "attention with weak direct competition".

## B. Knitting gauge mismatch / sweater sizing / positive ease

### Attention evidence
Recent Reddit beginner questions receive meaningful engagement:
- Jan 2026: gauge mismatch / size adjustment question — 185+ upvotes.
- Aug 2026: beginner unsure whether sweater is already too large — 111+ upvotes.
- Jan 2026: positive-ease discussion — 260+ upvotes.

Public YouTube tutorials on changing sweater pattern size for a different gauge have accumulated tens of thousands of views.

### Search evidence
- Public keyword list source reports `knitting gauge` around 1,300 monthly searches (source quality is secondary, not primary Google data).
- Amazon niche research reports meaningful annual search demand around knitting gauge measurement tools.

### Competition evidence
The problem initially looked underserved, but further SERP inspection found many active calculators and tools:
- stitchmath.com gauge calculator
- knitgrader.com gauge / ease content
- knitcalcs.uk sweater ease calculator
- multiple new general-purpose gauge calculator sites
- an iOS knitting gauge app

### Observation
There is strong attention, but the tool/SEO supply is now clearly developing.

### Decision
**Moderate opportunity, but no longer classified as low-obvious-competition.**

- Sarah Veblen Pants Quick-Reference Fitting Guide — $10.

### Decision
**Good demand; competition too obvious for the current target.** A narrower subproblem could still be tested later.

## D. Hand-pulled noodles / Lamian dough troubleshooting

### Attention evidence
- Serious Eats hand-pulled noodle video: 500k+ views.
- NYT Cooking hand-pulled noodle video: 160k+ views.
- Jun 2026 Reddit: Lanzhou lamian dough elasticity failure — 26+ upvotes.
- Jul 2026 Reddit: user asks why dough keeps tearing / will not become properly pullable.
- A long-running StackExchange question on failed pulled-noodle dough has ~10k views.

### Search / parent-demand evidence
- Public Amazon query data shows `hand pulled noodles` around 500 monthly Amazon searches; this is **not Google volume** and should not be treated as total market demand.
- Google results contain current 2026 recipe/content production and large mainstream-media interest.

### Paid competition evidence
In this scan, Etsy searches did **not surface an obvious direct paid troubleshooting guide** comparable to sourdough or pants fitting. Search results are primarily free recipes/tutorials.

### Opportunity gap
The recurring user problem is not "give me another noodle recipe" but:
- dough will not stretch;
- dough keeps snapping;
- dough relaxes once and then tightens again;
- flour protein differs by country;
- hydration/rest/alkali guidance conflicts across recipes.

### Cross-market advantage
Strong potential: hand-pulled noodles are a mature Chinese craft, and Chinese-language technique knowledge may contain practical distinctions not well organized for English-speaking home cooks. However, flour protein, alkali products, climate and ingredient labeling must be localized.

### Decision
**Promising low-competition candidate, but market-size risk remains.** Needs quantitative Google cluster validation before promotion to top candidate.

## E. Bao / Chinese steamed bun failure diagnostics

### Broad-market demand evidence
A public Semrush page for a US food site reports:
- `bao buns` — ~60,500 US monthly search volume (Jun 2026 dataset shown publicly by Semrush).

A separate BusinessWire release cites third-party market research claiming >196,000 monthly North American bao searches; because this is secondary marketing material, it is treated only as supporting context, not primary evidence.

### Audience-attention evidence
- Aug 13, 2026 Reddit / r/Baking: "What causes steamed buns to wrinkle up like this?" — ~474 upvotes, with hundreds of upvotes on likely-cause comments.
- Major bao recipe videos have large audiences:
  - Seonkyoung Longest bao video: ~1.7M views.
  - Souped Up Recipes steamed bao video: ~1.65M views.

### Problem structure
Repeated failure modes visible across current search results:
- wrinkled / elephant-skin surface;
- collapse after opening lid;
- dense / not fluffy;
- spreading sideways;
- yellow/brown spots;
- tearing / leaking filling;
- proofing uncertainty;
- metal steamer vs bamboo steamer behavior;
- condensation / thermal shock.

### SERP observation
Exact failure queries are often answered as **sections inside generic recipes or FAQ pages**, rather than by dominant dedicated troubleshooting brands/products.

Examples include Wagamama, Dished by Kate, recipe blogs, and other general bao pages. Dedicated problem pages exist, but the SERP does not appear to be dominated by established specialist products.

### Paid competition evidence
Etsy search surfaced mostly:
- generic bao recipe PDFs (~$2–$5),
- vegan bao recipe downloads,
- "easy bao" recipe downloads.

During this scan, no obvious mature Etsy category for **bao troubleshooting / steamed-bun failure diagnosis** surfaced.

### Cross-market advantage
Very strong.
Chinese home cooking has deep practical knowledge around:
- 发面 / 醒面;
- proofing state rather than fixed time;
- kneading and degassing;
- flour strength;
- steam intensity;
- lid opening / thermal shock;
- condensation control;
- seasonal temperature/humidity adaptation.

These concepts cannot be translated blindly; target-market flour protein, yeast types, kitchen temperatures, steamer equipment and recipe goals must be revalidated.

### Current hypothesis
A possible knowledge gap exists around:

> **"My bao / steamed buns came out wrinkled, collapsed or dense — what actually happened and what should I change next batch?"**

### Decision
**Highest-priority candidate from this scan for full validation.**

Reason: compared with the other candidates, it currently has the best combination of:
- large parent search demand;
- very recent high-engagement failure discussion;
- visual / diagnostic problem structure;
- weak obvious direct paid competition;
- strong cross-market knowledge-source advantage.

This is still a hypothesis until cluster-level Google demand and paid-conversion evidence are validated.

# Important unknowns before build

For Bao / steamed-bun diagnostics, we still need:

1. Google keyword-cluster volume for failure queries (not just `bao buns`).
2. SERP review for at least 10 problem queries:
   - bao buns collapsed after steaming
   - steamed buns wrinkle after steaming
   - bao buns not fluffy
   - bao buns dense
   - bao buns yellow spots
   - bao buns spread sideways
   - bao buns tough / chewy
   - bao dough not rising
   - bao buns tearing
   - how long to proof bao by temperature
3. 30–50 real user failure questions from Reddit, YouTube comments, cooking forums and recipe comments.
4. Direct payment evidence: are users willing to pay for troubleshooting, or only for recipes/courses?
5. A localization/source map comparing Chinese steamed-bun technique with Western target-market ingredients/equipment.
6. Food-safety boundaries for fillings/storage; initial product may deliberately focus on dough/steaming diagnostics to reduce risk.
