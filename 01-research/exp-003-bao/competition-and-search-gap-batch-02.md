# EXP-003 — Competition & Search Gap Batch 02

Date: 2026-09-07

## Question
Does Bao / steamed-bun failure diagnosis have a real content gap, or are we merely failing to notice existing dedicated troubleshooting competitors?

## Search patterns checked

- bao bun troubleshooting
- steamed bun troubleshooting
- bao buns collapsed after steaming
- steamed buns wrinkled
- bao buns not fluffy
- bao buns dense
- bao buns proofing
- mantou dense / wrinkled / collapse

## Observed SERP structure

### 1. Dedicated troubleshooting brands are not obvious
Search results frequently surface:
- generic recipe pages with a troubleshooting FAQ;
- blog articles with a small “what went wrong” section;
- Reddit / forum threads;
- cooking-community questions;
- appliance-specific steaming guides.

Examples observed in this batch:
- Dished by Kate: generic bao recipe with a “Why are my bao buns not fluffy?” section.
- Cheers Jenn: bread-machine bao recipe with troubleshooting FAQ.
- Umamicart (2026): general recipe with a troubleshooting section.
- What To Cook Today (updated 2026): no-yeast steamed-bun recipe with a dense/not-fluffy troubleshooting section.
- EatHealthy365 / Cook Like Mom: broad guides with troubleshooting tables.

### 2. Fresh content is still being created in 2026
Observed new/updated pages in 2026 explicitly adding troubleshooting sections for:
- dense / heavy buns;
- collapse / wrinkling;
- color issues;
- filling leakage;
- Instant Pot / steamer-specific failures.

This suggests the problem cluster remains active, but also means competition may be increasing.

### 3. Search-result weakness is organizational, not informational
The web contains many individual answers. The apparent gap is that answers are fragmented across recipes and forum threads, often with conflicting explanations.

Potential gap hypothesis:
> A dedicated diagnostic system organized by visible failure state could reduce decision time better than recipe-by-recipe FAQs.

This is an INFERENCE, not proven willingness-to-pay.

## Paid competition check

Searches for `bao troubleshooting`, `bao bun troubleshooting`, `steamed bun troubleshooting`, and Etsy variants did not surface a clear mature category equivalent to sourdough troubleshooting flowcharts/trackers.

What did surface:
- generic bao recipe PDFs;
- unrelated Etsy products due query ambiguity;
- free recipe troubleshooting sections.

### Observation
Direct paid competition appears weaker than sourdough.

### Caution
“Not surfaced in current search” is not equivalent to “does not exist.” We need a more systematic Etsy/Gumroad/Amazon scan before assigning Level A/B payment evidence.

## Competition rating — provisional

**Competitive but penetrable / possibly open at the dedicated-diagnostic-product level.**

Rationale:
- information competition = medium/high;
- dedicated troubleshooting-brand competition = currently appears low;
- broad `bao recipe` competition = high;
- exact failure-query SEO difficulty = UNKNOWN.
