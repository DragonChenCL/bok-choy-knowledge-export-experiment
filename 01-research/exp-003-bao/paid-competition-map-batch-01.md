## What did NOT surface clearly

During this search batch, no obvious established paid category surfaced for products explicitly positioned as:
- Bao Troubleshooting Flowchart
- Steamed Bun Failure Diagnosis
- Bao Rescue Guide
- Why Did My Bao Collapse / Wrinkle diagnostic printable
- proofing/steaming decision tree specifically for bao

Gumroad / Payhip searches did not surface a strong obvious dedicated product during this batch.

## Observation

There is **direct payment evidence for Bao knowledge in digital/PDF form**, but the paid products surfaced are mostly recipes and flavor variants rather than diagnosis systems.

## Payment evidence classification

### For “people pay for Bao digital knowledge”
**Level A-ish / direct format-category evidence**: paid bao recipe PDFs and cookbooks exist.

### For “people pay specifically for Bao troubleshooting / failure diagnosis”
**Level B / adjacent evidence only**: no direct dedicated troubleshooting seller/category was confirmed in this batch.

Do not claim direct willingness-to-pay for the proposed diagnostic product yet.

## Competition interpretation

- Generic recipe competition: **high**
- Flavor-specific recipe PDF competition: **medium/high**
- Free troubleshooting information: **medium/high but fragmented**
- Dedicated paid failure-diagnosis product competition: **appears low in current scan, not proven absent**

## Strategic implication

If this experiment proceeds, it should not compete as another “easy bao recipe PDF.” The candidate differentiation would need to be:

`visible failure -> likely variable(s) -> confirmation check -> next-batch adjustment -> reassess`

rather than `ingredients -> steps -> serve`.

## Remaining evidence needed

1. Search more niche marketplaces / book descriptions for explicit troubleshooting products.
2. Obtain direct purchase/conversion evidence only after a minimal test exists.
3. Do not set price yet; current competitor prices only establish that low-cost digital bao content is sold.
