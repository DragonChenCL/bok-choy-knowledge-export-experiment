# EXP-003 — Cross-Market Source Map Batch 01

Date: 2026-09-07

## Question
Can Chinese steamed-bread / mantou knowledge create a real source advantage for an English bao troubleshooting product?

## Authoritative / technical source evidence

### A. Optimized northern-style Chinese steamed bread process
Source: Journal of Cereal Science / ScienceDirect
URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0733521083710374
Observed parameters in the published procedure include:
- fermentation temperature around 32°C;
- relative humidity around 85%;
- explicit proof time;
- explicit steam-generation / steam-time control;
- sheeting identified as a critical processing variable.

### B. Chinese steamed bread sourdough / proofing research
Source: ScienceDirect
URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590259819300755
Observed experimental process includes proofing around 33°C / 75% RH and steaming with boiling water in a bamboo steamer.

### C. Northern Chinese steamed bread research
Source: PMC / Food Science research
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7037005/
Observed experimental process controls sourdough fermentation, proofing temperature (~35°C), RH (~80%), proof time and steaming time.

### D. US-based flour / steamed-bread study
Source: Oxford Academic, International Journal of Food Science & Technology
Observed process: proofing to a volume target (~1.9x) at controlled temperature / RH before steaming.

## Observation
Chinese steamed-bread research treats many variables that English home recipes often compress into a simple time instruction:
- flour characteristics / protein
- hydration
- mixing/kneading
- sheeting / degassing
- fermentation temperature
- proof temperature and humidity
- proof endpoint / volume
- steaming intensity/time
- cooling / post-steam handling

## Potential source advantage
Instead of translating Chinese recipes, we may be able to convert mature process knowledge into an English home-kitchen **diagnostic model**:

`failure symptom -> likely process variable -> confirmation check -> adjustment next batch`

## Localization gates — mandatory
Chinese research parameters must **not** be copied as universal home advice. Every transferable claim needs to account for:

1. flour type / protein differences;
2. instant vs active dry vs fresh yeast;
3. US cups vs metric weights;
4. target product: mantou vs filled baozi vs folded gua bao;
5. bamboo vs stainless / electric steamer;
6. climate / kitchen temperature and humidity;
7. batch size and home equipment;
8. food safety for fillings;
9. terminology (`bao`, `baozi`, `mantou`, `gua bao`, `steamed buns`).

## Working inference
Cross-market source advantage is plausible and stronger here than in many generic knowledge-product ideas, because China has both traditional process depth and formal food-science research on steamed wheat products.

Confidence: **Medium-high** for source availability; **unknown** for whether this advantage converts into buyer willingness.
