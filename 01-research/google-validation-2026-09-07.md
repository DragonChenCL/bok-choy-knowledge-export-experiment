# Google / Multi-Path Validation — 2026-09-07

## Trigger
User challenged the early signal: the most recent highly relevant Reddit pest question found in the first sample was in June 2026. Question: does that imply Bok Choy pest content has a very small audience?

## Rule
Do **not** infer market size from Reddit posting frequency. Reddit measures voluntary public discussion, not total demand. Validate through additional independent paths.

---

## Path A — Google SERP / content supply

### Queries checked
- `bok choy pests`
- `bok choy leaves holes`
- `why is my bok choy dying`
- `bok choy aphids`
- `bok choy flea beetle`
- `pak choi pests`
- `pak choi leaves holes`

### Observations
1. Google surfaces authoritative and commercial/gardening content dedicated to Bok Choy / Pak Choi pest problems.
2. Fresh pages continue to appear in 2026, including:
   - Rutgers Plant & Pest Advisory, May 22 2026: reports flea beetles especially prevalent in bok choy and other cole crops.
   - ShunCy, updated June 27 2026: dedicated `bok choy pests` article.
   - Almanac Bok Choy page updated recently with pest/disease troubleshooting.
   - AgricultureID Pak Choi page updated Sept 2 2026.
3. SERPs also include university Extension pages (NCSU, UF/IFAS, UMass, UKY), which proves the problem is established but also means free authoritative supply is strong.

### Inference
Google confirms a persistent problem space and active content competition. It does **not** yet prove that a Bok-Choy-only paid product has enough demand.

## Path B — Freshness / seasonality

### Evidence
Rutgers published a May 2026 IPM update specifically noting flea beetles in bok choy. UMass states flea beetle feeding has seasonal generations and that bok choy is highly attractive and susceptible from planting to harvest.

### Inference
Pest questions are seasonal. A gap between public Reddit posts can reflect season/geography and platform behavior rather than absence of demand.

---

## Path C — Base-crop demand proxy (not Google search volume)

### Evidence
Public MerchantWords Amazon keyword data currently shows US Amazon search volume for `bok choy` around 4,600/month and `pak choy` around 700/month. This is **Amazon search**, not Google search, and must not be presented as Google demand.

Source:
- https://www.merchantwords.com/search/us/choy

### Inference
The crop itself is not obscure to the point of having no consumer demand. However, pest-specific demand is necessarily a subset.

---

## Path D — Google Trends availability and limitation

Google Trends is an appropriate validation path because Google states it uses a normalized sample of actual Google searches. However, low-volume terms can appear as `0`, and this research environment did not expose a reliable term-level time-series for our exact Bok Choy pest queries in this pass.

Sources:
- https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en
- https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4355000?hl=en

### Decision
Do not fabricate or infer exact monthly Google volume. We still need reliable keyword-volume data (Keyword Planner / Ahrefs / Semrush / another transparent keyword source) before deciding the Bok Choy-only scope is commercially large enough.

---

## Path E — Broader category evidence

A current PlantFix article updated Aug 18 2026 targets `garden pest identification` and explicitly organizes diagnosis by **visible damage** rather than alphabetic pest lists. UC IPM also sells/maintains vegetable pest identification card products/resources for gardens and small farms.

Sources:
- https://plantfix.org/guides/garden-pests
- https://ipm.ucanr.edu/about/books-and-other-printed-educational-resources/

### Inference
The symptom-first decision structure is not just our invention; current market participants and institutional resources use/validate the same problem-solving pattern. The broader `garden pest identification` market is clearly larger than Bok Choy alone.

# Current conclusion

## What is supported
- Bok Choy / Pak Choi pest problems are real, recurring, seasonal, and still actively covered in 2026.
- Google SERPs contain both authoritative and fresh commercial content.
- The crop has meaningful base consumer interest.
- Symptom-first pest identification is a validated information structure.

## What is NOT supported yet
- That `Bok Choy Pest Rescue` alone has enough search volume to support a standalone paid knowledge business.
- Exact Google monthly search volume for the pest-related keyword cluster.

## Updated risk
**Market-size risk: Medium-to-high until keyword-volume evidence is collected.**
