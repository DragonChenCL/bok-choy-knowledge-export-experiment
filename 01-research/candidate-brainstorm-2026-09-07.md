# Candidate Brainstorm — 2026-09-07

## Objective
Identify a better EXP-002 candidate than Bok Choy for validating the reusable topic-validation SOP.

## Selection constraints
A candidate should ideally have:
- recurring, concrete user problems;
- evidence of current demand in public discussions/search;
- existing willingness to pay for digital knowledge/printables;
- low compliance / credential risk;
- enough free authoritative material to cross-check claims;
- a problem structure that can be compressed into a decision aid, checklist, guide, tracker, or printable;
- multiple acquisition paths (SEO, marketplaces, communities, Pinterest/short video where relevant).

## Candidate A — Sourdough Starter Rescue / Troubleshooting

### External evidence
- Reddit has fresh 2026 beginner troubleshooting posts around starters not rising, cold kitchens, feeding ratios, and uncertainty about whether the starter is dead or recoverable.
- Etsy currently shows 68+ results for `sourdough troubleshooting`.
- Existing Etsy products demonstrate strong format-level payment evidence: examples include troubleshooting PDFs, starter trackers, beginner guides, and an interactive troubleshooter. Search results include products with 947, 1.4k, and 1.5k review counts in adjacent starter/tracker categories.
- A recently listed 2026 troubleshooting PDF sold at $6.29 and received a review saying it helped solve real problems; another interactive decision-tree product has 10 reviews.
- Google SERPs contain both major authority content (e.g. King Arthur Baking) and newer 2026 independent troubleshooting pages, suggesting demand is active but competition is real.

### Problem structure
Strong symptom-first queries:
- starter not rising
- starter bubbling but not rising
- starter stopped rising
- starter smells like acetone / spoiled milk
- hooch on starter
- starter weak after fridge
- cold kitchen starter
- false rise / quiet days
- when to discard vs keep feeding

### Product hypothesis
**Sourdough Starter Rescue Kit** — a beginner-oriented decision system:
`What you see/smell -> likely cause -> what to check -> what to change -> when to reassess -> when to restart`.

Possible assets:
- 60-second diagnostic flowchart
- starter symptom decoder
- feeding-ratio decision sheet
- cold-kitchen adjustment sheet
- rescue log / tracker
- "save, wait, or restart" checklist

### Risks
- Strong competition and abundant free content.
- Food-safety-related claims (especially mold/contamination) must be carefully sourced.
- Generic ebook positioning is weak; differentiation must come from decision compression and usability.

### Early score
**High priority for full SOP validation.**

## Candidate B — FDM 3D Print Failure Troubleshooting

### External evidence
- Fresh 2026 Reddit posts and new independent sites are actively publishing guides for stringing, warping, layer separation, under/over extrusion, ghosting, Z-banding, wet filament and related failures.
- Etsy has current paid 3D-printing guides and troubleshooting ebooks; examples include a $9.42 2026 guide and cheaper beginner guides with reviews.
- The problem is naturally diagnostic and highly structured.

### Product hypothesis
**3D Print Failure Diagnostic Atlas** — visual symptom -> likely causes -> one-variable-at-a-time test -> slicer/printer fix.

### Advantages
- Very clear decision-tree structure.
- Buyers already spend meaningful money on hardware/filament, so failed prints have direct time/material cost.
- Strong SEO long-tail inventory.

### Risks
- Excellent free troubleshooting content already exists.
- Printer/slicer/material combinations create complexity and maintenance burden.
- Advice can age as slicers/firmware change.

### Early score
**Medium-high priority.** Strong problem, but harder to produce a reliably universal guide.

## Candidate C — Home Espresso Dial-in Troubleshooting

### External evidence
- r/espresso remains active in 2026; recent posts still revolve around dialing in, sour/bitter shots, dose/yield/time confusion, and repeatability.
- Etsy currently shows 10+ `espresso dial in tracker` listings; an Espresso Brew Log / Dial-In Tracker has 500+ reviews and a $12 price point. Adjacent coffee tasting/log categories show hundreds to thousands of reviews.

### Product hypothesis
**Espresso Dial-In Decision Tree + Shot Log** — taste/problem -> which variable to change next -> record the result.

### Advantages
- Expensive hobby: users have strong motivation to reduce wasted beans and failed shots.
- Recurring use, not just one-time reference.
- Clear printable/tracker product format with proven payment behavior.

### Risks
- Results vary by grinder, machine, bean, roast level, temperature, pressure and user taste.
- Expert communities already provide strong free guides.
- A generic worksheet is commoditized; product needs a stronger decision framework.

### Early score
**Medium-high priority.** Attractive, but requires careful technical nuance.

## Candidate D — Compost Troubleshooting

### External evidence
- Community discussions consistently revolve around smell, moisture, heat, maggots, slow decomposition, and brown/green balance.
- Problem structure is suitable for a simple diagnostic chart.

### Risks
- Weak payment signal relative to sourdough/coffee.
- Users may be less willing to buy a guide because the economic/emotional cost of failure is lower.

### Early score
**Low-medium priority.** Do not select before stronger wallet evidence.
