# Evidence Standard

## Rule
No material action moves forward only because it sounds reasonable or because an LLM already knows it.

## Evidence tiers

### Tier A — preferred for factual guidance
- University Extension
- Government / public agricultural institutions
- Primary platform listings and official documentation
- Primary research where relevant

### Tier B — useful for market / user-language evidence
- Reddit and other direct community discussions
- Etsy / Gumroad product listings
- Search-result composition
- Independent specialist sites

### Tier C — hypothesis generators only
- AI-generated suggestions
- Generic blogs without primary sourcing
- Unverified social posts

## Decision rule
For any meaningful claim that affects product scope, pricing, safety, channel choice, or SEO strategy:
1. Gather at least two independent signals where practical.
2. Prefer at least one Tier A source for factual agricultural claims.
3. Distinguish **observed fact**, **inference**, and **hypothesis** in logs.
4. If evidence is weak or contradictory, do not finalize the decision.

## Safety boundary
This experiment will not invent pesticide recommendations, rates, or pre-harvest intervals. Region-specific chemical guidance must come from current authoritative local sources and product labels. The initial knowledge product should emphasize diagnosis, inspection, cultural/physical controls, and IPM-oriented decision support.
