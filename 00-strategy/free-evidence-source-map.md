# Free Evidence Source Map

> Purpose: Define the default free evidence stack used before any paid SEO data source is considered.

## Principle

A topic is not validated by one platform. Each source answers a different question, so we triangulate across multiple free signals.

## 1. Google SERP / web search

Use for:
- whether people phrase and search the problem in recognizable ways;
- which pages currently rank;
- whether results are dominated by major authorities or include niche independent sites;
- whether current answers are generic, fragmented, outdated, or buried inside FAQs;
- whether recent pages and discussions continue to appear.

Record:
- query;
- top result types;
- freshness;
- content format;
- obvious gaps;
- whether a dedicated troubleshooting page/product exists.

Important limitation:
Google SERP evidence does **not** provide reliable exact monthly search volume by itself. Exact volume remains UNKNOWN unless a quantitative source is available.

## 2. Reddit / forums / Q&A

Use for:
- real user wording;
- repeated problems;
- what people already tried;
- confusion and conflicting advice;
- decision urgency;
- recency and engagement proxies such as votes/comments.

Community evidence proves pain and language, not factual truth.

## 3. Recipe/article comment sections

Use for:
- long-tail questions that may never become standalone Reddit posts;
- repeated failure modes across years;
- questions from people who actually followed a recipe and failed;
- localization variables such as flour, temperature, steamer type, batch size, equipment, substitutions.

These comments are particularly valuable because the user has already attempted the task.

## 4. YouTube

Use for:
- parent-topic audience scale;
- whether instructional content attracts sustained attention;
- comment questions and failure themes;
- format potential for future acquisition.

Video views are an audience proxy, not direct proof of Google search demand or willingness to pay.

## 5. Etsy / digital marketplaces

Use for:
- whether buyers already purchase PDFs, printables, trackers, recipes, flowcharts, or guides in the category;
- price ranges;
- reviews/sales signals where visible;
- whether the exact troubleshooting product exists or only adjacent products exist.

Payment evidence levels:
- Level A: same problem/outcome is already paid for.
- Level B: adjacent category/format has paid demand.
- Level C: only free content found.

## 6. Academic / government / professional sources

Use for:
- validating mechanisms and technical facts;
- separating correct advice from community guesses;
- identifying variables that actually affect outcomes;
- building a trustworthy knowledge base.

For food topics, research may cover flour properties, fermentation, proofing, temperature, humidity, steaming, starch/gluten structure, etc.

## 7. Cross-market source research

Use for:
- finding mature knowledge in another market (for this experiment, Chinese mantou/bao technique);
- identifying techniques, diagnostics, and mental models that may not be well organized in the target English market.

Every transferred idea must be revalidated for:
- climate;
- ingredients;
- equipment;
- units;
- user goals;
- cultural/food expectations;
- safety/regulatory context.

## What this free stack can prove well

It can usually establish:
- problem existence;
- problem recency;
- repeated pain clusters;
- user language;
- SERP competition structure;
- content gaps;
- adjacent willingness to pay;
- parent-category attention;
- factual mechanisms;
- plausible acquisition channels.

## What it cannot reliably prove

Without a quantitative keyword database or first-party Search Console data, we should not invent:
- exact monthly volume for every long-tail query;
- exact KD;
- exact CPC;
- exact total addressable search traffic.

Those values remain UNKNOWN until a reliable source is available.

## EXP-003 examples

For Bao / Steamed Bun Failure Diagnosis we used:
- Google/web SERPs for queries such as `bao buns not fluffy`, `steamed buns wrinkled`, `bao buns collapse after steaming`, and related terms;
- Reddit discussions about wrinkling, collapsing, rough surfaces, dense texture, proofing, and steamer problems;
- Red House Spice troubleshooting pages and reader comments;
- YouTube bao/steamed-bun tutorials with large view counts as parent-market attention proxies;
- Etsy digital bao recipe PDFs as adjacent payment evidence;
- academic Chinese steamed-bread research as a technical and cross-market knowledge source.

## Default rule

Use the free evidence stack first. Pay for quantitative SEO data only when the decision materially depends on precise volume/KD/CPC, such as comparing two close candidates or forecasting larger SEO investment.
