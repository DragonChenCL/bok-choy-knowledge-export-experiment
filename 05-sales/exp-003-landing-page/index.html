FAQ

Before you buy

Is this a recipe?

No. It is a troubleshooting guide designed to work alongside the yeasted Bao / steamed-bun recipe you already use.

Does it cover every steamed-bun tradition?

No. Version 1 focuses on common yeasted wheat steamed-bun dough failures across folded Bao / Gua Bao and filled Baozi. No-yeast-only and sourdough-specific methods are outside scope.

Will it give me one exact proofing time?

No. Kitchen temperature, yeast activity and dough formula change proofing speed. The guide emphasizes observable proof state.

Is this based on anonymous Reddit advice?

No. Community posts help identify recurring problems. Technical guidance is cross-checked against established baking sources and food-science literature.
