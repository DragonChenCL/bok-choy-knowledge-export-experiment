## FAQ

### Is this a recipe?
No. It is a troubleshooting guide designed to work alongside the Bao recipe you already use.

### Does it cover every regional steamed bun style?
No. Version 1 focuses on common yeasted Chinese-style steamed buns / bao and the most repeated failure states found in the research.

### Will it tell me an exact proofing time?
Not as a universal rule. Proofing speed changes with kitchen temperature, yeast activity, dough formulation and other variables. The guide focuses on observable state and diagnostic checks.

### Is this based on personal restaurant experience?
No. We do not claim experience we do not have. The guide is a research-driven diagnostic product built from cross-checked technical sources and recurring real-user failure patterns.

### Does it include food-safety guidance for fillings?
Only general scope notes. Meat and other perishable fillings should be handled and cooked according to appropriate food-safety guidance; the product is primarily about dough, proofing and steaming failures.
