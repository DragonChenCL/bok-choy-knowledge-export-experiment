## Full product description

You followed a Bao recipe.
The dough rose.
The buns looked promising.

Then they wrinkled, collapsed, came out dense, barely puffed, or behaved differently from your last batch.

Most Bao recipes explain what to do **when everything goes right**.

**Bao Rescue is built for the moment when it doesn't.**

Inside the guide:

- a 60-second symptom finder;
- a wrinkle / collapse decision tree;
- dense / not-fluffy diagnosis;
- under-proofed vs ready vs over-proofed cues;
- steamer and condensation checks;
- flour / hydration troubleshooting without fake country-to-country flour equivalence;
- a one-variable next-batch worksheet;
- a printable 1-page quick diagnostic.

### What makes it different

This is not another recipe collection.

The value is **decision compression**:

`what you see` → `what to check` → `likely cause families` → `one controlled change next batch`

Community posts and recipe comments were used to discover real user failure patterns. Technical guidance was cross-checked against stronger baking and food-science sources.

We do not claim restaurant experience we do not have, and we do not sell "secret Chinese tricks."

### Scope
Version 1 focuses on common **yeasted wheat steamed-bun dough failures** across folded Bao / Gua Bao and filled Baozi.

It does not attempt to cover:
- every regional steamed-bun tradition;
- no-yeast-only formulas;
- sourdough-specific steamed buns;
- detailed filling formulation;
- meat-filling food-safety instructions.

For meat or other perishable fillings, follow appropriate food-handling and cooking guidance.
